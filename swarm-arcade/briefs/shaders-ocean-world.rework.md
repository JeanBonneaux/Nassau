## Orders (fragment shader + camera constants; keep JS structure, presets, input, adaptive scaler)
1. Camera framing: the horizon must sit at about 40% from the bottom of the frame, sea filling the lower ~45-55%. Change pitch to `clamp(-0.12 + (uMouse.y-0.5)*0.5, -0.3, 0.25)` (currently looks too high; the old formula at idle gives sky only). Idle autopilot (mouse.y ~ 0.5) must give the framing above, also in preview. Verify visually; adjust until the sea fills the lower half.
2. Bigger, more dramatic waves: in wave3/wave2x raise base amplitude from 0.85 to 1.6, and make wavelengths shorter: frequencies 0.18, 0.324, 0.5832 -> 0.30, 0.52, 0.85 (and the wave2x ones x1.4). Keep the sharp-crest trick. Increase the distance fade slightly: exp(-dist*0.012) so mid-distance swells stay visible. Make the ocean surface travel (time multipliers x1.3).
3. Water colour must be rich, not grey-green: uDeep dusk = (0.01,0.07,0.13), uLight = (0.05,0.38,0.42); `refr *= 0.8+0.3*dot(n,sun)` fine, but add a subsurface glow on crests facing the sun: `refr += uLight*pow(max(dot(n,normalize(uSunDir+vec3(0,0.3,0))),0.0),4.0)*hn*0.5`. Use `fres` with exponent 4 and range `*0.9+0.04`, reflection multiplier 0.6 -> 0.9 so the sunset sky and the sun's long glitter path read strongly on the water. The sun specular: keep the power 300 term (x8) and add a broad warm path term `pow(rs,12.0)*0.6`.
4. Horizon: stop the far sea fading to a pale band. Fog colour should be `mix(uHor, uZen, 0.15)` and strength `1.0-exp(-t*0.006)`; plus keep the dark island silhouettes but make them larger (ridge amplitudes x2.5) so a distinct dark line breaks the horizon. The sun should sit just above the horizon (sunDir y about 0.07-0.12 for dusk) so the glitter path is long and the sky glows orange.
5. Sky richness: zenith deeper (0.03,0.11,0.24), horizon hotter orange (1.0,0.50,0.25); clouds: add a second layer (another fbm lookup at a different scale and speed, e.g. uv*1.7+vec2(5.2,1.3)+time*0.035 weighted 0.5) and light the cloud undersides orange/pink (`uCloud*sunC`) so the sky has clear colour bands rather than a beige wash. Reduce overall exposure slightly (`1.0-exp(-col*1.1)`) to avoid the pastel/washed look; keep vignette.
6. Horizon stair-stepping: it comes from the low internal resolution and the hard `if(d<0.01)` edge. Fix by (a) default scale 0.7 (min 0.4, max 1.0 in the adaptive scaler), preview 0.4; (b) in water() blend toward fog by distance (item 4), which hides the aliased edge; (c) when the march misses within the last steps near the horizon, fall through to sky (already does). Also make the march a touch more robust: `t += clamp(d*0.6, 0.05, 6.0)`, 56 steps non-preview (preview 32), max t 600.
7. Interaction check: hold-to-ascend (camera rises to ~9) must still look good (sea fills more of the frame, waves read as a field of swells); T presets: noon and night must each show a visible sea too (the night preset needs the moon glitter path; make sure water is not black: raise night uLight to (0.03,0.14,0.24)).
