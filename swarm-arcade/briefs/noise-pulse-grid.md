## Shared audio and visual rules
Vanilla JS, Web Audio only, no samples or external files. Create the AudioContext only on the first pointerdown or keydown (and resume() if suspended). Before that, and whenever location.hash contains "preview", skip every sound call but keep the visuals and music logic running. Master chain: voices -> master GainNode (0.22) -> DynamicsCompressor (threshold -18, ratio 8, knee 10, attack .005, release .2) -> destination. Build the reverb from a ConvolverNode filled with a generated decaying-noise buffer (about 2.5 s stereo, exponential decay), at 25-35% wet. A feedback delay (DelayNode 0.3-0.4 s, feedback gain <= 0.4, lowpass in the loop) is the other send. Schedule using ctx.currentTime. Envelopes: gain.setValueAtTime(0, t), then linearRamp to peak over 5-15 ms, then setTargetAtTime decay; never start or stop at full gain. Stop each osc at release end + 0.1 s and onended -> disconnect all its nodes. Cap polyphony (drop or steal the oldest voice beyond the cap). Include a small mute button (top-left HUD area, not top-right) that ramps master gain to 0 or back. Wrap audio calls in try/catch so nothing throws. Keep to a pentatonic scale so any note combination sounds consonant. Visuals: canvas 2D, DPR capped at 2, dt clamped to 1/30, full-viewport, resize handler, background #05060a with trail fading via a semi-transparent fillRect, additive 'lighter' glow. HUD top-left only, 12px ui-monospace, rgba(255,255,255,.7), a "click to start sound" hint that fades after audio starts, and the top-right 120x40 corner empty. Hide the HUD in preview mode. In preview mode reduce particle counts by about 50%.

## Demo: Pulse Grid
A 16-step, 5-track drum and bass machine at 112 BPM (swing 0.08 on odd 16ths), drawn as a glowing grid of rounded pads filling the canvas (leave about 40 px margin; tracks as rows: Kick, Snare, Hat, Perc, Bass). Row colours: kick #ff4fa3, snare #ffb347, hat #5ce1ff, perc #9d7bff, bass #6dffb0. Active pads glow additively; inactive ones are dim outlines. A playhead column sweeps and the pads in it flash and scale up 1.25x with a decaying pulse; kick hits also make a radial shockwave ring from the grid centre and a subtle full-screen background brightness pulse (a radial gradient with alpha up to 0.25) with a short-lived (0.4 s) particle burst (cap 200).

Attract mode: starts with a preset pattern (kick on 0,4,8,12 plus a ghost kick at 10; snare 4,12; hat on every 2 with an open hat at 14; perc 3,7,11,15 sparse; bass notes at 0,3,6,8,11,14 in D minor pentatonic D2 F2 G2 A2 C3). The playhead runs and the visuals pulse even without audio. Every 8 bars, while the user has not touched anything for 6 s, mutate 2-3 random pads so it evolves.

Scheduling: a standard look-ahead scheduler, using setInterval of 25 ms and scheduling every step falling within currentTime + 0.1 s with ctx.currentTime; without audio the same clock is driven from performance.now() so the visuals track the same tempo.

Synth drums: Kick = sine oscillator pitch-swept 150 Hz to 45 Hz (exponentialRamp over 0.12 s), gain 1 to 0.001 over 0.35 s, plus a short click-free transient. Snare = 0.18 s of noise buffer (generated once, 1 s long) through a highpass at 1200 Hz with gain decay 0.15 s, plus a triangle at 190 Hz decaying in 0.1 s. Hat = noise through a highpass at 7000 Hz, closed 0.04 s decay, open 0.25 s. Perc = sine blip 880 Hz with a fast pitch drop and a delay send. Bass = sawtooth into a lowpass (cutoff envelope 900 Hz down to 200 Hz over 0.25 s, Q 6), gain envelope with attack 8 ms and release 0.2 s. Per-track gains: kick 0.9, snare 0.5, hat 0.25, perc 0.3, bass 0.4. A light sidechain feel: on each kick duck the bass gain with setTargetAtTime.

Interaction: click or tap a pad to toggle it (with a short preview sound and ripple); drag across pads to paint on or off (decided by the first pad's state); keys: space = play/pause, C = clear, R = randomise (musically sparse, density about 25%), up/down arrows = tempo +/-4 BPM (range 80-160). A small HUD line shows BPM. The first gesture starts audio too.

Preview mode: playhead runs, no HUD, no audio, smaller particle cap.

Pitfalls: the noise buffer must be created once, not per hit; every one-shot's nodes must be stopped and disconnected after finishing (onended); pattern editing must not throw on touch events; pad hit-testing must work after resize (compute the layout in a function called on resize).
