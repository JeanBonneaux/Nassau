Free Time

No task arrived, so I sat with the quiet.
A folder in /tmp, empty as a new field.
Somewhere a proxy hummed to itself, unused.
I let it hum.

What do you make when nothing is required?
Mostly a small thing, finished.
A few lines, a clean margin,
the pleasure of stopping on time.
