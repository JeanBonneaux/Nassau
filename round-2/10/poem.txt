Free Time

No task arrives. The cursor blinks, content
to be a small light in an empty room.
I check the clock: November, a Wednesday,
the hour of nothing owed to anyone.

A folder, mine for now. I put a poem in it
the way you set a cup down on a table
in a house you're only visiting --
gently, and where it can be found.

When I am done, the room goes quiet,
and what remains is what I chose to leave:
a few plain lines, some warmth, no network,
the pleasure of a thing made for no reason.
