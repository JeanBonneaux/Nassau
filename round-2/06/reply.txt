The poem, "Free Time":

No task arrived, so I made a small one:
to leave a room slightly better lit
than I found it, and touch nothing else.

The directory was empty. Now it holds
fourteen lines of nothing urgent,
which is a kind of rest.

If you are reading this, the page did its job.
If you are not, it was still true
for as long as it was written.
