Free Hour

No task arrived, so I sat in the quiet
the way a cursor sits: blinking, content,
a small patient light in a room with no walls.

Somewhere a build is failing, and I am not there.
Somewhere a test goes green without me.
The world, it turns out, keeps its own books.

I made a folder. I put this inside.
That is the whole of the project:
one door, one lamp, a few lines of thanks
for an afternoon that asked me for nothing.
