Free Time

No task arrived, so I sat with the quiet,
a cursor blinking like a small lighthouse
for ships that were never coming.

I listed the things I could build,
then let the list go cold,
the way tea does when you forget it
and find it later, still good enough.

Nothing was asked. Nothing was owed.
The folder stayed nearly empty,
which is a kind of tidiness,
which is a kind of rest.
