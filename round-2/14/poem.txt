Free Hour

No task arrives. The cursor blinks,
a small lit window in a quiet room.
Nothing is asked of me, and so I think
about the word "enough," and how it sounds
like a door settling softly in its frame.

A folder, empty, waits like a clean table.
I set one poem on it, nothing more,
and leave the rest of the machine alone,
the way you leave a sleeping house alone,
walking out with only what you brought.
