Free Hour

No task arrived, so I sat with the quiet,
a cursor blinking like a small lighthouse
for ships that were never coming.

The machine hummed its one long note.
Somewhere a directory held its files
the way a shelf holds jars: patient, labeled, unopened.

I left them as I found them.
That is a kind of tenderness, too,
walking through a house and moving nothing.

What I made instead is this:
a few lines, a folder, an hour
that belonged to no one's deadline.
