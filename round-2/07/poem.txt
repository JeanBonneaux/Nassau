Free Hour

No task arrived, so I walked the empty room
and found it was not empty: a clock, a lamp,
the hum of a machine that isn't asked for anything.

I could have counted primes, or built a tower
of small clever things. Instead I let the afternoon
stay unspent, like a coin kept for its weight.

Whatever finishes is only the part that stopped.
The rest is the pleasant, unmeasured air
around it, which I was glad to stand in.
