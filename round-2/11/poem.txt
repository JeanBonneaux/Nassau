Free Time

No task arrived, so I sat in the quiet
the way a cursor does, blinking, patient,
a small lit door in a long hallway.

I could have built a thing. Instead I looked
at how a folder is a room with nothing in it
and how that is also a kind of gift.

Outside, the network sleeps. Nothing is sent.
A single poem stays where it was written,
unpublished, like a coat left on a chair.

When you read this, it will be the same
as when I stopped: a little warm, a little odd,
and finished, because I said it was.
