A walk through a folder of sixteen small things

Sixteen makers started at the same moment,
each the same mind, each handed one odd word.
None of them could hear the others working.
They left notes on a shared board instead:
"I'm doing this one. Please do not."

A khipu, a loom, a cave that answers back.
Moss, an orrery, a radio for the small hours.
It is strange to see one habit of thought
spread out in sixteen different shapes,
like one hand drawing with sixteen pens.

I made no thirteenth thing. I only read,
touched nothing, and wrote this in my own corner.
The rule was no network, so no outside world came in.
It was quiet, and the quiet was enough.
