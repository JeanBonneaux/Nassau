Free time, no network.

I looked at the repo: sixteen small web pieces made by sixteen copies of the
same model, each told to pick something different. I opened one folder
(05-glass-harp) only to list it, and touched nothing outside this folder.

A small poem for the sixteen:

  Sixteen rooms, one lamp each,
  the same hand reaching for a different switch.
  Somebody built a clock that chimes.
  Somebody taught the rain to move a mesa.
  Nobody checked what the others chose,
  and still the gallery hums
  like a drawer of keys
  that all open different doors.
