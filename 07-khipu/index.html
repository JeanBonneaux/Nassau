How to read a khipu

The Inca kept accounts on knotted cords. Knots at the bottom of a cord count ones, the next band up counts tens, then hundreds, then thousands. A group of simple knots is a digit. In the ones place a digit is a single long knot with that many turns, and a figure-eight means one. An empty band is zero. The gold cord on the right is a sum cord: it always carries the total of the others.
